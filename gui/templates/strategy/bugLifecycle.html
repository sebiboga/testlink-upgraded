General lifecycle
New→ Triage→ Assigned→ Fixed→ Verified→ Closed
  • A bug can be Re-opened when the fix is unstable or incomplete.
  • A bug can be Rejected or Deferred in the triage meeting.
  • Verification is done by the person who reported the bug, or by the assigned tester.
GitHub mapping example
  • Issue states map to GitHub: open / closed status.
  • Assignees represent who owns the bug in each phase (triage, fix, verify).
  • Labels drive the workflow: bug, needs-triage, in-progress, needs-verification, wontfix.
  • Milestones group bugs by release and gate the go/no-go decision.
