What is ISTQB?
The International Software Testing Qualifications Board (ISTQB) is the leading vendor-neutral certification body for software testing. The Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) syllabus is the most widely recognised entry-level reference for testing principles, test design techniques and test management practices used across the TestLink workflow.
